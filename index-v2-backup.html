What your site is costing you — Las Vegas ATV Tours

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a visitor planning an ATV ride would: search, open a result, check the tour and price, book. Your price, tour details, phone number and booking button are easy to find. The leaks are a very heavy homepage, slow-loading pages, an address that only appears in the page code and years in business that don't match.

Checked October 17, 2026

What I found

01

Your homepage carries an 11.9 MB photo.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, the homepage loaded about 19 MB and the main content showed at about 69 seconds on both runs (speed score 31 and 33 out of 100). One photo in the gallery block, ATV-photo-1-1.jpg, is 6000 by 4000 pixels and about 11.9 MB, and it is shown in a box 220 pixels tall (180 on a phone). The test estimates that sizing images properly would save about 14.6 MB. Your tour page loaded about 5.3 MB (speed score 37 and 34) and the contact page about 5.0 MB (33 and 53). One test setup, two runs per page; field speed is unmeasured.

02

Pages stall for seconds while scripts load.

In the same load test the pages were blocked from responding for 3,820 and 2,100 milliseconds on the homepage, 1,620 and 2,280 on the tour page and 5,410 and 550 on the contact page. The test flagged third-party code, unused JavaScript and scripts that block the first paint. I didn't trace which scripts belong to which tool.

03

Your address isn't on any page a visitor can read.

On the pages I opened, the visible text gives a phone number and an email but no street address or map. The address, 6845 Speedway Boulevard, Las Vegas 89115, appears only in the page code. The homepage says tours are “20 minutes from the Strip near the Las Vegas Motor Speedway”. I didn't open the booking system, which may show a meeting point.

04

The business details in the page code are incomplete.

The phone number is written “+7027509751”, which starts with +7 rather than +1. The map position (latitude and longitude) is two empty strings, and the opening hours are an empty list. Your Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, TripAdvisor and Yelp profiles are linked on the page but not listed in the markup. The business is also described in two separate blocks.

05

Your years in business don't match.

The About page says “Since our inception in 2004”, the homepage says “With over two decades of experience”, and your blog has one post titled “Nears 20 Years Of Desert Adventure” and another titled “18 Years Of Adventure”. I can't tell which number you want visitors to see.

06

Some labels and buttons are hard to use.

The three photos in the homepage gallery are labeled “Top”, “Bottom” and “Main” for screen readers. The orange (#ff5a1f) badges with white text score 3.11 contrast where 4.5 is expected. Two cookie-notice links have no readable name, and the privacy-policy link in the footer is 17 pixels tall. My accessibility check scored the homepage 89, the tour page 89 and the contact page 88 out of 100.

07

A few page-level slips.

The Blog page has no preview image for sharing, and the Privacy Policy page has no description and four main headings (the Groups page has three). The homepage has a “LEARN MORE” link that doesn't say what it leads to, and the “More” menu toggle is a link with no address. The search-engine check scored the homepage 85 and the other pages 92 out of 100.

What's working

Your price ($136.74 with taxes and fees), the two-hour length with one hour of riding, the 16-and-up rule and a Book Now button are on the homepage and the tour page, and a search tool quoted the price back. Your phone number is a tap-to-call link on every page and nearly all the pages I opened have their own title and description, and every one has a canonical tag. Your robots.txt doesn't block AI crawlers, you publish an llms.txt and a sitemap that lists your pages with dates, and the site runs over a secure connection. Every image has alt text, and you have group, FAQ and blog pages plus links to your TripAdvisor, Yelp, Google Maps and four social profiles.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether the booking system and contact form reach you, where guests meet your guides, how many Google reviews you have right now, and whether the “#1 rated” and “2,000 five-star reviews” claims match your listings. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt doesn't block any AI crawler I tested. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked. It does block a separate list of other crawlers, such as Baiduspider and Yandex.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML contains about 1,600 words of text and one clear heading (“LAS VEGAS ATV TOURS”), so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Found
    Price and tour facts are in plain text

    The homepage and tour page state $136.74, two hours with one hour of riding, ages 16 and up and what's included, and a search tool quoted the price in its answer.

  • Note
    An llms.txt file exists

    www.lvatvtours.com/llms.txt loads as text. It is written as a JSON-style list of quoted lines rather than plain sections, so I can't say whether AI tools read it as intended.

  • Gap

    The markup has your name, address and price range, but the phone is “+7027509751”, the map position is empty, the opening hours are an empty list and no profile links are listed.

  • Gap
    Address only in the page code

    The visible pages show a phone and email but no street address. The markup says 6845 Speedway Boulevard, Las Vegas 89115.

  • Found
    Profiles are linked

    The page links your Google Maps listing, TripAdvisor, Yelp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 17, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure
